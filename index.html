Samira Abedini

Research Assistant, CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information SecurityMSc Computer Science student, Saarland University

I am a Master's student in Computer Science at Saarland University and a research assistant in the Dormant Neurons group at CISPA. My research focuses on the safety and security of LLM agents. I'm particularly interested in how agents influence each other and how understanding these interactions and their cascading effects can help us build safer and more robust multi-agent systems. Recently, I've also been exploring self-improving multi-agent systems and their safety implications : )

Try it
All agents agree. Click on each to make it stubborn.

These agents listen to each other. Purple: right answer, orange: wrong one. Make one agent stubborn, and it gradually misleads the others, creating an influence cascade. Turn on the defense, and the agents learn not to trust the stubborn one. But how can we model these influence cascades and make multi-agent systems more robust against them? That's the kind of problem I'm working on :)

Projects

AgentChair: a conference run entirely by AI agents

An interactive challenge built for the BIFOLD Berlin Summer School on AI and Society. Participants attack the reviewer agents to flip paper decisions, then work out how to defend against those attacks.

Try it2026
MAS-Challenge: prompt injection against a multi-agent system

A CTF-style challenge run at summer schools in Vienna and Cagliari. Over 2,000 attack attempts were made, and only one participant cleared the hardest level.

Try it Code 2025
Fine-tuning MolFormer Chemical Language Model

Fine-tuned the model to predict molecular lipophilicity. Used influence functions (LiSSA) and uncertainty-based selection to choose the training data, and compared BitFit, LoRA and IA³ fine-tuning. Built for the NNTI course project.
